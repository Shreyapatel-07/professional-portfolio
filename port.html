About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian aerospace scientist, author and teacher. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a simple family and developed a strong interest in science and education from an early age. He studied physics at St. Joseph's College and later studied aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

Dr. Kalam worked with important Indian scientific organisations such as ISRO and DRDO. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. Because of his contribution to India's missile programme, he became popularly known as the "Missile Man of India". He also served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and was popularly called the "People's President".

Dr. Kalam was a great teacher and an inspiration to millions of students. He encouraged young people to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals. His famous book Wings of Fire continues to inspire students. He passed away on 27 July 2015 while giving a lecture to students.

Major Contributions

1. SLV-III Programme

Dr. Kalam was the project director of India's SLV-III programme and played an important role in launching the Rohini satellite.

2. Missile Programme

He made major contributions to India's Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme.

3. Pokhran-II

He was associated with India's strategic technology programmes during the Pokhran-II tests in 1998.

4. Technology Development

He promoted science and technology for the development and progress of India.

5. Youth Inspiration

He inspired millions of students to dream big and work towards their goals.

6. Books and Education

He wrote several books and spent much of his time motivating students.
